## Projects

### 📉 [S.U.P.E.R. BET: Early Warning System](https://github.com/paulc02/datasprint-2026-bet-ews)
Built at the DataSprint 2026 datathon (ASE × KPMG), where our team won the "Risk Radar" special award. Estimates every day the probability that the BET index falls at least 5% in the next 20 trading days, by stacking logistic regression, LightGBM and a survival model under walk-forward validation, with SHAP explanations. Comes with an [interactive dashboard](https://paulc02.github.io/datasprint-2026-bet-ews/). In a historical simulation, following its alerts would have cut the portfolio's maximum drawdown from 31% to 16%.
`Python` `TypeScript` `machine-learning` `early warning system`

### 💳 [Loan Default Risk Analysis](https://github.com/paulc02/loan-default-risk-analysis)
Predicts which of 307,511 loan applicants are likely to default. Combines SQL feature engineering, logistic regression and LightGBM (AUC 0.765), with a [Tableau dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/views/loan-default-risk-dashboard/Portfoliooverview). Rejecting the riskiest 10% of applicants would avoid about 35% of defaults while losing under 8% of good clients.
`Python` `SQL` `Tableau` `default analysis` `machine-learning`

### 📈 [BVB Energy Volatility Forecasting](https://github.com/paulc02/bvb-energy-volatility-forecasting)
Bachelor's thesis project. Models and forecasts the volatility of three Romanian energy stocks (OMV Petrom, Nuclearelectrica, Hidroelectrica) and the BET-NG index using ARMA, GARCH and GARCH-X models, testing whether macro variables (oil, gas, FX, ROBOR) improve the forecasts. Daily data, 2012–2026, with out-of-sample evaluation.
`Python` `time series` `GARCH` `forecasting`

### 🏦 [Bank Customer Churn Prediction in R](https://github.com/paulc02/bank-customers-churn-prediction)
Predicts which of 10,000 bank customers are likely to leave. Compares fuzzy clustering, logistic regression, decision trees, KNN and a neural network. The neural network performed best (AUC 0.864), and age emerged as the strongest churn factor.
`R` `classification` `clustering` `churn analysis`
